Before we start

A few fair questions.

Is my budget too small?

Tell me what you have in mind and what you’re comfortable spending. We can discuss a smaller first version, agree on the essentials, and leave room to add more later. You’ll know the scope and price before committing.

Do we need to be local to each other?

No. I’m based in Westminster, Maryland, and work remotely with clients nationwide. We can plan, review, and hand over your project online.

What happens after I get in touch?

I’ll read your brief and reply personally. We’ll talk through what you need, what already exists, and a realistic scope. If it’s a good fit, I’ll put the proposed work and pricing in writing.
